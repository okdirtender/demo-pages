What Is a Timeline? 🕰️

A timeline puts life's moments in order —
oldest first, newest last — so you can see the whole journey at once.

  • Think of it like a family scrapbook, laid out in a line
  • Each dot or card is one important moment
  • You can use it for family history, a recipe, a holiday plan…
  • …or just to celebrate someone's life 💛
