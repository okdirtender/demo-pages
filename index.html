Tips for Timeline Slides

  • Use fragment classes to reveal events one at a time
  • Use data-auto-animate to smoothly grow the connecting line
  • Keep labels short — the slide is not a document
  • Choose vertical for depth, horizontal for brevity
  • Progress bars work well for Gantt-style roadmaps
